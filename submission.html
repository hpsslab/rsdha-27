SUBMISSION

Submission instructions for RSDHA 2027 will be announced, including paper formats, review details, deadlines, and the submission link.

HPCAsia 2027 conference proceedings will not include workshop papers. Any separate RSDHA publication arrangements will be announced here.

Requirements for publishing workshop proceedings:

  • Authors are invited to submit original papers in two separate tracks:
    • Full Paper: submit regular research papers in a two-column format with at least 8 pages and at most 10 pages in length, which include tables, figures, and references.
    • Short Paper: submit initial results and position/work-in-progress papers in a two-column format with at least 4 pages and at most 6 pages in length, which include tables, figures, and references.
  • All submissions should be formatted according to the ACM Proceedings Style listed in the HPCAsia 2027 website (https://hpcasia2027.org/2027/pages_submission/call_for_papers.vm).
  • Solicitation for papers will be open and impose a formal peer review process.
  • Papers will be carefully reviewed by a diverse program committee composed of experts from academia, National Labs, and industry.
  • Reviewing will be done in double blind, therefore, DO NOT include any author names or affiliations on any submitted documents and DO NOT reveal the author or affiliation information via any action such as metadata, previous work.
  • Accepted full papers will be published in ACM-affiliated proceedings. (Short papers will not appear in the proceedings.)
  • At least one author of an accepted paper must register for and attend the workshop.
  • Please submit papers through the RSDHA 2027 EashChair site: https://easychair.org/conferences?conf=rsdha27

The workshop will take place on Friday, February 19, 2027, from 9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. (KST, UTC+9), at Yonsei University in Seoul, Republic of Korea.
